## Image Requirements

- Format: JPG or PNG
- Recommended size: 800x800px or larger
- File size: Keep under 500KB for fast loading
- Aspect ratio: Square (1:1) works best for product cards

## Tips

- Use high-quality images with good lighting
- Make sure product is centered and clearly visible
- Consider adding a subtle background or border
- Test how images look on both desktop and mobile
